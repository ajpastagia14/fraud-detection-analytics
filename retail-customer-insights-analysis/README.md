# Retail Customer Insights & Business Intelligence Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail customer purchasing behavior using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify sales trends, customer segments, and business performance indicators.

The objective is to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights that can support strategic decision-making, customer retention initiatives, and revenue growth opportunities.

## Business Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing patterns
* Identify high-value customer segments
* Measure sales performance trends
* Evaluate product category performance
* Generate business recommendations through data-driven analysis

## Tools & Technologies

* SQL
* Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)
* Power BI
* CSV Dataset

## Analysis Workflow

1. Data Cleaning and Validation
2. Exploratory Data Analysis
3. SQL-Based Business Queries
4. KPI Development
5. Dashboard Design and Visualization
6. Business Insights and Recommendations

## Key Deliverables

* Customer Segmentation Analysis
* Revenue Trend Analysis
* Product Performance Dashboard
* Interactive Power BI Dashboard
* Executive Summary Report

## Business Recommendations

Based on customer purchasing behavior, organizations can:

* Improve targeted marketing campaigns
* Increase retention of high-value customers
* Optimize product assortment strategies
* Enhance customer engagement initiatives
* Support data-driven business planning
